SnuggleTP – /snuggle teleports you directly to your partner. Cooldown optional.

CuddleBeds – If both of you sleep in beds within X blocks, it gives a bonus (regen for 30s, etc).

Private Chests – Chests tagged with your name, only you can open.

Silly Titles – Custom /title commands like “Snow the Silly Fox” above your name.

Shared Hearts – Both share a “bonded heart bar” (if one eats, both heal a little).
